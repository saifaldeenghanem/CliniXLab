2. High Pressure Alarm Differential (PIP vs. Pplat via Inspiratory Hold) & DOPE Emergency Protocol

A. High PIP + Normal Pplat (Wide Gradient > 5–10 cmH2O)

Problem = High Airway Resistance (Raw ↑): Endotracheal tube or bronchi are narrowed, while alveoli are normal.
• Causes: Bronchospasm (Asthma/COPD), Mucus plug/secretions, Patient biting ETT, Kinked tubing, Water in circuit.
• Action: Suction ETT, give Nebulized Bronchodilators, insert Bite Block / sedate.

B. High PIP + High Pplat (Narrow Gradient, Both ↑↑)

Problem = Decreased Lung/Chest Compliance (Cstat ↓): Stiff lungs or compressed thoracic cavity.
• Causes: ARDS, Cardiogenic Pulmonary Edema, Tension Pneumothorax, Atelectasis, Right Mainstem Intubation, Abdominal Compartment.
• Action: ARDSNet low Vt (4–6 mL/kg) to keep Pplat < 30 cmH2O; needle decompression if Tension Pneumothorax!

C. The ICU "DOPE" Mnemonic for Sudden Deterioration

First Step: Disconnect from ventilator & manually bag with 100% O2 via BVM!
• D — Displacement: ETT cuff leak, extubation, or Right Mainstem migration.
• O — Obstruction: Mucus plug, biting tube, kinked circuit.
• P — Pneumothorax: Tension pneumothorax (Immediate 2nd ICS MCL decompression!).
• E — Equipment: Ventilator failure, disconnected O2 source, or stacked Auto-PEEP.

3. Master Reference Matrix: Ventilator Modes (Fixed vs. Variable), Weaning (RSBI < 105) & VAP Bundle

Mode Trigger / Limit / Cycle Fixed (Guaranteed) Variable (Must Monitor!) Clinical Pearl & Nursing Vigilance
VC-CMV (Volume A/C) Time/Pt | Volume | Volume Tidal Volume (Vt) & Flow PIP & Plateau Pressure (Pplat) Guaranteed Minute Ventilation; monitor PIP (<35) and Pplat (<30 cmH2O) to prevent barotrauma.
PC-CMV (Pressure A/C) Time/Pt | Pressure | Time Inspiratory Pressure (Pinsp) & Ti Exhaled Tidal Volume (Vte) & VE Protects lungs from high pressure, but if compliance drops or Raw rises, Vte drops sharply → acute hypercapnia!
PRVC (Dual Control) Time/Pt | Pressure | Time Target Vt at Lowest Pressure Auto-adjusted Pinsp breath-by-breath Hybrid mode delivering target Vt with decelerating flow; ideal for dynamic lung compliance.
SIMV + PSV Sync Mandatory + Spont PS Mandatory RR & Vt/Pinsp Spontaneous Vt & Total RR Always combine SIMV with Pressure Support (PS ≥ 8–10 cmH2O) to overcome ETT resistance.
PSV / CPAP (SBT) Patient | Pressure | Flow (25%) Set Pressure Support & PEEP RR, Vte, and Ti (100% Patient) Set Apnea Backup! Extubation ready if RSBI = RR / Vt(L) < 105, strong cough/gag, and positive Cuff Leak Test.

Weaning & Extubation Readiness Criteria (SBT 30–120 min)

• Reversible cause improved, Hemodynamically stable (minimal/no pressors), SpO2 ≥ 92% on FiO2 ≤ 40% & PEEP ≤ 5–8 cmH2O.
• Rapid Shallow Breathing Index (RSBI = RR / Vt in Liters) < 105 breaths/min/L (e.g., RR 20 / 0.45 L = 44 → Pass!).
• Intact airway reflexes (cough, swallow, gag), GCS > 8, and Positive Cuff Leak Test (audible air leak around ETT when cuff deflated).

The 6-Point ICU VAP (Ventilator-Associated Pneumonia) Bundle

1) Head of Bed Elevation 30°–45°, 2) Daily Sedation Vacation & Daily SBT Assessment, 3) Oral Care with Chlorhexidine (0.12%) q4–6h, 4) Continuous Subglottic Suctioning & ETT Cuff Pressure 20–30 cmH2O, 5) Peptic Ulcer Prophylaxis (PPI / H2 blocker), 6) DVT Prophylaxis (LMWH / SCDs).
